About Us

Nearly 20 years of medical device consulting expertise

Who We Are

We're a Jacksonville-based consulting team with nearly two decades of hands-on experience in the medical device industry.

Having spent close to 20 years developing, launching, and managing innovative technologies at leading medtech companies, we understand every step of the journey — from concept and design to regulatory approval and market success.

Our mission is simple: to empower startups and established organizations to bring life-changing medical technologies to patients faster, smarter, and more affordably.

What Drives Us

We're deeply passionate about innovation that improves lives.

Over the years, we've seen how great ideas can stall — not because of poor science, but because founders lack the technical guidance, compliance know-how, or operational support to move forward efficiently.

That's why we created our firm: to help medical device entrepreneurs and small companies bridge that gap with practical expertise, scalable systems, and cost-conscious strategy.

Our Philosophy

We believe innovation shouldn't be limited by funding or complexity.

Our approach blends industry experience, startup agility, and genuine collaboration — ensuring every project we touch moves closer to improving patient outcomes.

Where We're Based

Located in Jacksonville, Florida, we're part of a growing medtech ecosystem alongside Mayo Clinic innovators, university incubators, and emerging startups.

We proudly support founders across Florida — from Gainesville to Orlando to Palm Coast — and partner with clients nationwide through virtual and on-site collaboration.
